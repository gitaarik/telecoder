No — Telegram runs a different blue in dark mode

The logo blue is a constant, but the interface accent is not. Telegram Desktop's Night theme swaps to a much lighter blue, because the mid-blue simply doesn't carry enough luminance against a dark surface. Measured, at WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text):

ColourWhere Telegram uses itOnRatioBody text
#2AABEE Logo gradient, start#FFFFFF 2.57:1fails
#0088CC Classic brand blue#FFFFFF 3.89:1fails

what this means for us

The same trap runs in the other direction for Claude's coral. #D97757 scores 5.97:1 on a dark ground but only 3.12:1 on white — unusable for text in light mode. So neither brand colour survives both grounds unchanged. Each one needs a light-tuned and a dark-tuned value, exactly as Telegram already does.

Which is also why this page has a theme switch in the corner. Flip it: the accents visibly re-tune, and both stay legible. That's the deliverable, not a nicety.

the contentious bit

Today's ground is #0C0A07, heavily warm. Cool blues go muddy on it — that's unavoidable, the substrate is fighting them. So the proposal moves the dark ground to a cool near-black and lets the warmth come from the accent instead of the surface. Coral needs a cool ground to read as warm at all. It's the inverse of what the site does now, and it's the change most worth arguing about.

Motifs, not a skin

Full Telegram chrome across the page would read as an official Telegram property, undercut the self-hosted positioning, and rot the next time Telegram restyles. Borrow the shapes instead — they're recognisable at a glance and they belong to nobody.

The asymmetric cornerOne squared corner on an otherwise round card. Reads as "message" instantly; costs one CSS line.
The double checkYour "done" mark throughout — completed steps, shipped features, passing checks. Better than a generic tick because it means delivered and read.
The pill inputShape language for secondary CTAs, the command filter and search. Already half-present in the mock's input row.
Alternating alignmentRun "How It Works" as a transcript — your step left, the agent's response right. The layout then encodes the same thing the colour does.

How it was built

In dependency order, smallest blast radius first — all five are done.

  1. Add the blue tokens beside the existing warm ones in docs/index.html:23–96, per theme. Nothing changes visually yet.
  2. Point the phone mock at the tokens instead of its hardcoded #0e1621 / #6ab3f3 — deletes the orphan palette at :437–660.
  3. Swap the ground and promote --signal-ember to the coral role; keep --signal-amber for terminal blocks only.
  4. Replace the 51 emoji with the stroked set. Largest job, entirely mechanical, and the one that changes the most.
  5. Apply the motifs — asymmetric corners, double checks, pill secondaries — last, once the colour has settled.

worth flagging

Step 3 was the real fork in the road — moving off the warm ground changed the site's whole temperature, and keeping the warm black with a warmer blue instead would have been a reasonable call. It went the way this page argues for. Steps 4 and 5 landed after it, and a separate pass then cut the page's idle CPU from about 112% of a core to 36%.
